About me

I'm Sebastian Wellin, an audio engineer, producer and songwriter based in Stockholm with over 10 years of experience working across genres — from indie and jazz to extreme metal and hardcore punk.

I got my audio engineering degree with first class honors from SAE Institute Stockholm and Middlesex University.

My studio has a high quality, professional recording rig that is completely mobile, meaning I can come to you to record! The only requirement is electricity. Combined with Pro Tools, a great selection of microphones and a growing collection of outboard and plugins. Remote work is fully supported — I deliver stems, sessions and revisions via WeTransfer or Dropbox.

  • Pro Tools
  • Logic Pro
  • Mastering
  • Live sound
  • Podcast editing and mixing
  • Foley / sound design
